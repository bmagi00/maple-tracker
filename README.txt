MAPLE TRACKER
EXP, mesos and items per hour for MapleStory Classic
=====================================================

It reads the numbers off your screen, the same way you'd read them with
your eyes. It never touches the game files or the game's memory.

The safest setup is a second PC (or laptop) that watches a Discord stream
of the game, so nothing extra runs on the PC you play on. It also works
when pointed straight at the game on the same PC, if you prefer.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Unzip the whole MapleTracker folder somewhere, e.g. your Desktop.
   Keep all the files together - the app needs them.

2. Double-click MapleTracker.exe.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't
   signed by a company. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. Put the game (or the Discord stream, fullscreen) on screen and click
   Calibrate in the tracker window. Move your mouse onto that screen.
   After a 5-second countdown you'll see a screenshot. Drag boxes around:
     - the EXP numbers on the bottom bar, e.g. 12345[12.34%]  (needed)
     - the meso total in your inventory window                 (optional)
     - the area where "You have gained..." messages show up    (optional)
   Draw each box tightly around the text, then press Enter.

4. The app then shows what it read. The numbers should match the game.
   If not, click the gear icon > Calibrate and try tighter boxes.

5. Click Start and go grind. Drag the tracker window anywhere, as long
   as it isn't covering one of the boxes you drew.


WATCHING A DISCORD STREAM ON A SECOND PC
----------------------------------------
- On your game PC, Go Live with just the game window, at the best
  quality you have.
- On the second PC, open the stream, pop it out and make it fullscreen.
- Keep the mouse off the stream. Discord's controls cover the bottom of
  the video, which is where the EXP bar is.
- Don't minimize the stream.


SEE YOUR STATS ON ANOTHER DEVICE
--------------------------------
The bottom of the tracker shows an address like http://192.168.1.50:8765
Open it in a browser on any PC or phone on the same Wi-Fi. You can
start, pause and reset from there too.
The first time, Windows will ask whether to allow MapleTracker on the
network. Allow it on Private networks.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Per-hour numbers show up after the first minute.
- Calibrate again if you change game resolution, stream quality or
  monitors.
- Gear icon (or right-click the tracker) for: Calibrate, Check reading,
  Open data folder, Quit.
- Your settings, session logs (sessions folder) and tracker.log are saved
  next to MapleTracker.exe. If that folder isn't writable, they go to
  %APPDATA%\MapleTracker instead.
- If something breaks, send tracker.log to whoever gave you the app.
