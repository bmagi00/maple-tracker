Get set up

  1. Share the window that shows the game. On this second computer, that's your Discord, YouTube or Twitch stream.
  2. Keep the game's bottom bar in view. The tracker finds LV and EXP by itself; rates start 15 seconds later.
  3. Mesos count by themselves from the pickup messages in the bottom right. Open your Item Inventory too (anywhere on screen) for an exact total.

Use this on a second device, not the PC you play on. Nexon's rules can treat screen-reading programs on the gaming PC as unauthorized third-party tools.
